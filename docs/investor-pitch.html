01 · Problem

Students Pay Full Price.
They Shouldn't Have To.

Millions of Egyptian students rely on private tutoring and online courses — but accessing discounts is manual, inconsistent, and easily abused. Teachers lose control. Students get frustrated.

😤

No Reliable Verification

Teachers offer verbal discounts with no system to verify students, leading to fraud and discount abuse by non-students.

📋

Manual & Error-Prone

Discount management is done via WhatsApp messages or paper lists. Codes are shared publicly and expire without tracking.

📉

Revenue Leakage for Teachers

Without a controlled system, teachers lose revenue to code sharing and have no data on discount usage or ROI.

🌐

No Scalable Platform

No Egyptian platform offers a unified, multi-school, multi-teacher discount management system in both Arabic and English.

04 · Business Model

Multiple Revenue Streams,
Low Operational Cost

EduDiscount operates as a SaaS platform with per-school subscription pricing, plus optional transaction-based and premium tiers. Infrastructure cost is minimal — it runs on a single VPS.

Primary · Recurring

School Subscription

Schools pay a monthly or annual fee to be listed on the platform and issue discount codes to their students. Includes admin dashboard, analytics, and student code management.

500–2,000 EGP/mo
Secondary

Per-Code Commission

A small percentage (1–3%) of the discount value is charged per successfully redeemed code. Aligns EduDiscount's revenue with teacher success.

1–3% per redemption
Expansion

White-Label Licensing

Schools or educational networks that want a fully branded, private deployment pay a setup fee plus annual license. Target: large school chains and universities.

5,000–20,000 EGP setup
Premium

API Integration
